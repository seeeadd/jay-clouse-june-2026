Day 2

The Pricing Worksheet

Price for renewal, not for the launch spike.
"Recurring revenue requires recurring value." (Me, to myself, a lot.) The price isn't what someone pays to join. It's what they re-decide, every single month, is still worth it.
From your Membership Audit: reachable · last month
1
The value sentence
Finish it: "A member who stays six months will have that they couldn't get anywhere else."
If you can't finish this, the problem isn't price.
2
The month-9 test
What does your member get in month 9 that they didn't get in month 1?
If your honest answer is "the same thing, slightly more polished," that's the leak. Members aren't paying for slightly-more-polished. They're paying for what compounds.
3

4
The renewal test
Would the member from Q1 re-buy at this price in month 6, with no discount, no bonus, no launch push?
5
Your starting point
$/month  or  $/year  ·  Tiers:
Start with fewer tiers than you think you need. Adding later is easy, removing is painful. Your starting price flows into your 30/60/90 roadmap.
